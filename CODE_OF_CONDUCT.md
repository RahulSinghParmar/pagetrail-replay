# Code of conduct

PageTrail Replay welcomes contributors with different backgrounds and levels of experience. Project spaces should remain respectful, constructive and focused on improving the software.

## Expected behaviour

- Communicate with patience and respect.
- Critique ideas and implementations, not people.
- Give useful context and accept good-faith correction.
- Respect privacy, confidentiality and intellectual property.
- Avoid sharing captured material that other people are not authorised to access.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, doxxing and publication of private information are not acceptable in project issues, pull requests, discussions or other project spaces.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainer through GitHub. Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, reject contributions or restrict participation when necessary to protect contributors and the project.

This policy applies whenever someone is representing or participating in the PageTrail Replay community.
